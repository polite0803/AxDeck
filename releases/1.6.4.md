This release moves the actions of the Services & tools tab into their own settings page.

Changed
- The panel tab now shows status only. Probing sites, starting and stopping services, installing packages and repairing readings live in Settings › Services and tools, reachable from the "Manage in settings" button at the bottom of the tab.
- Both places share one set of readings, and BGE-M3 and AxTV polling stops when neither the panel tab nor the settings page is visible.

这个版本把「服务与工具」页的操作移到独立的设置页。

变更
- 面板上的这一页现在只显示状态。探测站点、启停服务、安装应用包、修复读数等操作都集中在「设置 › 服务与工具」，从面板底部的「在设置中操作」进入。
- 两处共用同一份读数；面板页与设置页都不可见时，BGE-M3 与 AxTV 的轮询会停止。
