This release fixes a fault where a page could be left showing on top of the next one after switching tabs.

Fixed
- Switching pages in the expanded panel could leave the previous page on screen underneath the new one. Moving from the services page to the devices page, for example, kept the services page's environment rows and its row of buttons (Probe sites, SSH login test, Container status, Copy diagnostics…) drawn under "No wireless devices connected".
- Every page drew its own list of the pages to hide, and the lists no longer agreed: the devices, system monitor and clipboard pages never hid the services or desktop pages, and the music page never hid the services page, so a switch between those pages left the page being left behind visible. Each page's render now names only the page to show, and one place hides all the others, so a page added later cannot be forgotten.

这个版本修好了切换标签后旧页面的内容残留在新页面上的问题。

修正
- 在展开的面板里切换页面时，上一页可能仍然留在屏幕上、压在新页面的底下。例如从「服务/工具」页切到「设备」页，服务页的环境信息与那一排按钮（探测站点、SSH 登录测试、容器状态、复制诊断……）会一直画在「没有已连接的无线设备」上面。
- 每个页面各自写了一份「要隐藏哪些页」的清单，而这些清单彼此已经对不上了：设备页、系统监控页和剪贴板页从不隐藏服务页与桌面页，音乐页从不隐藏服务页，于是这些页面之间切换时，被离开的那一页仍然可见。现在每个页面只说明自己要显示，隐藏其余页面由一处统一完成，以后新增页面也不会再漏。
